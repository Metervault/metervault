About Nimba PTY LTD

Proudly South African smart water metering solutions for a sustainable future

Who We Are

Nimba PTY LTD is a proudly South African company dedicated to transforming how water is measured, managed, and conserved. With deep roots in local communities and a strong understanding of South Africa's water challenges, we deliver smart metering solutions that empower municipalities, estates, and industries to take control of their water usage.

Founded with a vision to address South Africa's critical water management needs, Nimba has become a trusted partner for organizations seeking innovative, reliable, and locally relevant water metering solutions.
