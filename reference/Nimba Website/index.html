Who we are

About Us

Nimba PTY LTD was established in Durban and formed as a company in 2020. All operations are conducted at our head office in Durban, but with a growing operational footprint and customer base, we have expanded our services across South Africa with dedicated teams, technical support, and service capabilities.

Positioning our business as a leader in smart water metering requires an unwavering commitment to sustaining excellence across all areas of our operation. As a business, we provide unquestionable value, quality and service in water management solutions.

We expand our market share by building a reputation with our customers and competitors that is characterised by integrity. In turn, this allows us to establish long term relationships with those who choose us as their smart water metering provider.

OUR PRIORITY

Health And Safety

Nimba PTY LTD recognises that the health and safety of its employees is critical to its ongoing sustainability and success, and with this in mind we maintain the highest standards of workplace safety and environmental responsibility.

As valued stakeholders, the safety of employees, contractors and the communities in which we operate, remains of the highest concern to the company. We strive to create a culture where employees assume an equal responsibility for their own safety within their working environment.

Our key objectives are to

  • Achieve and maintain the highest practical level of safety control in all areas of operations
  • Identify, manage and monitor hazardous risks in water infrastructure projects
  • Meet and where possible, exceed legal and regulatory requirements
  • Facilitate the training of both operational and technical personnel
  • Ensure compliance to safety and quality standards
  • Implement and maintain best practices in smart water metering
