Comprehensive Water Management Solutions

Nimba PTY LTD serves a diverse range of industries across South Africa, providing specialized smart water metering solutions that address the unique challenges and requirements of each sector. From municipal utilities to residential estates, we deliver technology that works.

Municipal Utilities

Smart Cities. Smart Water Management.

Municipal utilities face unique challenges in water management, from aging infrastructure to increasing demand and regulatory compliance. Our smart metering solutions help municipalities optimize their water networks, reduce losses, and improve service delivery to communities.

Key Benefits:

  • Reduced water loss and improved revenue collection
  • Real-time monitoring of water distribution networks
  • Automated billing and customer service
  • Leak detection and proactive maintenance
  • Compliance with water regulations and standards
  • Data-driven decision making for infrastructure planning

Recommended Solutions:

  • AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure) systems
  • Ultrasonic smart meters for high accuracy
  • Automated meter reading (AMR) networks
  • Pressure management and control systems
  • Water quality monitoring integration
